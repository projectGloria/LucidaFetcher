Test services
Lucida fronts several stores and its backends come and go without notice. Test asks each one for a track that certainly exists and reports what came back — a store answering "uh-oh!" is down at Lucida's end and will find nothing whatever you search for. Enabling a dead store costs a few seconds per skipped track and finds nothing.
