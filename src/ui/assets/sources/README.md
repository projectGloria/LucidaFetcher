# Source icons

These files were downloaded unchanged from service websites or the asset URLs
linked by those websites. Exact download URLs are recorded in sources.json.
SoundCloud's white compact logo comes from its official developer branding page:
https://developers.soundcloud.com/docs/api/buttons-logos

The images identify the current or successful audio provider. They are bundled
locally for offline use and retain their original colors and proportions.
Trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Lucida's own website
refused asset requests (HTTP 403); Lucida is a broker, while track rows identify
the provider such as Qobuz, Amazon Music, or SoundCloud.
